53,221 megawatt-years.

That is how far the connection dates on Britain's transmission queue moved later, net, between 19/07/2024 and 22/07/2025, counting only the moves the register's own stage labels settle. A 100 MW project whose date slips a year counts 100 MW-years.

For 88 project groups that were split, merged, renumbered or otherwise restructured, the register does not say which row became which. Paired one way they add 4,602; paired another, 8,322. So the year's total is 57,823 or 61,543 MW-years. We publish both, and neither is a bound.

Why stop at July 2025? The connections reform re-baselined every date after that, so later copies are a separate series. NESO overwrites the register with each new copy; this series is rebuilt from 694 of them back to 31/01/2014, from NESO's own disclosures, web archives and live captures. Anyone can request the same disclosures; we started earlier.

What the page offers is independently verifiable provenance, publication time and methodology suitable for scrutiny. The rules were sealed and externally timestamped before any figure was computed, every copy is listed by digest, and the corrections section is public.
