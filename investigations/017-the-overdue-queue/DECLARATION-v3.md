**The starting line and the next copy.** Written 2026-09-28, after the
schema pass over the copies the daily capture holds and before any census
figure was computed for any copy later than 15 September 2026. Frozen by
`scripts/freeze`, which witnesses these bytes with OpenTimestamps and
RFC 3161 tokens from freetsa.org and DigiCert and commits the file with its

## Reading rules

Version 1's rules R3 (dates), R4 (not Built), R5 (the measure) and R6 (the
unit and its second reading), and version 2's rules R8 to R12 (the Gate
cell, the tier, the blank, two shares, what the cross-tab can distinguish),
are **inherited verbatim** and apply to each copy on its own. R1 and R2
are restated, because they named one copy and one date.

**R1′ — the copies.** The reference copy is the one named above by digest;
if the mirror's bytes do not hash to it, the run refuses. The next copy is
the copy with the **earliest** `last_modified` on or after 2026-09-29 among
those recorded in committed daily manifests. The capture runs once a day,
so a copy NESO published and replaced before the capture's next run is
never seen; the census is of the first copy the capture holds, and the
page says so. If the manifests show any day from 2026-09-29 to that copy's
capture day with no TEC record, the gap is reported with the census. If no
such copy has been captured by 2026-10-31, no next census is run and that
is recorded as the outcome.

**R2′ — the as-of date, and NESO's filename.** Each copy's as-of date is
the date of its CKAN `last_modified`, the convention of every live pin in
the archive. A row's date has passed if its parsed `MW Effective From` is
strictly earlier than that copy's as-of date. NESO's filename also names a
date, and on the reference copy it names the day before (24 against 25
September). Where the two differ, the census publishes, as a declared
sensitivity, the rows and MW that are selected under the as-of date and
whose effective date is on or after the filename's date and before the
as-of date: the rows that are past on one reading and not on the other.

**N0 — the next copy's schema pass comes first.** Before the next copy's
census, `scripts/schema-report tec-capture` is rerun and committed with
that copy in it. The census runs only if the report shows, for that copy,
the same fifteen columns; date spellings only `uk` and blank; only the
five `Project Status` spellings above; only `1`, `2` and blank under
`Gate`; and no flag. If any of these fails, no census of that copy is
computed under this version. A reading for what changed is then declared
and witnessed as an amendment before any figure is computed, and is
written from the schema report alone.

## What each copy's census contains, declared before it is computed

For each of the two copies, separately:

- **Version 1's census** under R1′, R2′ and R3 to R6: the selected rows
  and MW under both readings, and exactly version 1's four breakdowns (by
  status as printed, by plant type as printed, by calendar year of the
  effective date, the ten earliest selected rows). It also includes R3's
  day-month sensitivity, R2′'s filename sensitivity, and version 1's two
  scale figures: MW dated on or after the as-of date, and of that the MW
  whose status is `Scoping`. No other breakdown may be added after a run.
- **Version 2's Gate cross-tab** G1 to G5 over that census's own selection,
  exactly as version 2 declares them.
- **G6′**: G6 extended. For each selected Gate 2 row, its Gate cell and
  published date in every gated copy up to and including the copy under
  census. These are the four gated copies version 2 read, and after them
  each captured copy later than 15 September, one reading per distinct
  digest. Rows are found again by `overdue_queue.identity_key` (the
  15-character project id with the stage), the rule version 2's page
  names. R12 and the gap of 19 May to 22 August 2026 that the FINDINGS
  correction of 2026-09-24 records apply unchanged. A row whose dates fall
  in that gap is reported as unsettled, never as having entered the tier
  already past.

## The comparison, declared before either census is computed

Computed only once both censuses exist.

- **D1 — side by side.** For each copy: the as-of date, rows and MW
  selected (both readings), the Gate 2 overdue rows and MW, and the scale
  figures; and the difference, next minus reference, of each. Every
  difference is printed with both copies' as-of dates, because the
  calendar alone moves rows into the selection as dates arrive.
- **D2 — row transitions.** Selected rows in either copy, matched by
  `identity_key`, classed as: **in both**; **reference only**, with what
  the next copy prints (no row with that key; `Built`; date on or after
  the next as-of date; or other, listed in full); **next only**, split into
  (a) no row with that key in the reference copy, (b) the same date in both
  copies, falling on or after the reference as-of date and before the next
  one, *the date arrived*, and (c) other, listed in full. A key that is on
  more than one row in either copy is listed as ambiguous and put in no
  class. Rows and MW are given for each class, and the classes must sum to
  each census's own totals.

## Checks each run must pass, or it fails

- **C1′ to C3′** Version 1's C1, C2 and C3 for each copy, against that
  copy's own row count and its `project_status` in the capture schema
  report.
- **C4′** The copy carries no flag in the capture schema report, including
  the partial-export rule against the captured copy before it.
- **C5′** Each copy's selected rows are written append-only, and a
  committed line that would change on recompute stops the run.
- **C6′ to C9′** Version 2's C6, C8 and C9 for each copy, against the
  capture schema report. Version 2's C7 is replaced: the cross-tab reads
  exactly the rows this version's census of the same copy selected.
- **C10 — the method check.** Before either census is computed, this
  version's runner is applied to the captured copy of 15 September, which
  has the same digest as the copy versions 1 and 2 read. It must reproduce
  version 1's committed selection line for line and its totals and
  breakdowns to the last penny. It must reproduce version 2's G1 to G6 to
  the last penny. If it does not, the runner is not the declared method
  and no figure of this version is computed.
- **C11** D2's classes sum to each census's selected rows and MW.

## Falsifiers, declared in advance

- **F1 to F6** as versions 1 and 2 declare them, for each copy on its own.
- **F7** If C10 fails, no figure of this version is published.
- **F8** If N0 fails for the next copy, no next census is published under
  this version, and the comparison is not run.
- **F9** If more than a tenth of either copy's selected rows are ambiguous
  or have no project id under `identity_key`, D2 is not published and the
  comparison is D1 alone.
- **F10** If R2′'s filename sensitivity on either copy moves more than a
  tenth of that copy's selected MW, that copy's headline is published as a
  range with both readings, never as one number.

## What this version never claims

Everything versions 1 and 2 refuse, and three more:

- **That any difference between the two copies was caused by the
  announcement of 29 September, by any policy, by NESO's connections reform
  or by the queue fee.** The copies are days apart. The register records
  dates and statuses, not reasons, and a change in either can have many
  ordinary causes.
- That rows classed *the date arrived* are late, or that rows leaving the
  selection were delivered. A row that leaves may have moved its date, been
  built, or been withdrawn, and D2 reports which of these the register
  prints, never which one happened.
- That either census measures the queue's health. Each measures what one
  copy of the register printed on its own as-of date.

## Order of work, and what is published

1. This file is frozen. 2. The runner for this version is written and
C10 passes. 3. The reference census is computed. 4. The next copy is
captured; N0's schema pass is committed. 5. The next census and the
comparison are computed. No step starts before the previous one is
committed. **Both censuses and the comparison are reported in FINDINGS.md
whichever way they come out.** Publication beyond the repository remains
the sponsor's act. Figures in the storage addendum stay outside this
version, and an outbound use of them still needs a declaration of its own.
