# EIR request to NESO: monthly terminations and cancellation charges since CMP376 (draft, not sent)

*Drafted 22/10/2026 under 025's declaration (J6, "validation to request, not to compute"). For the sponsor to send, or not; nothing is sent from the repository. The request asks for the one public-interest series that checks 025's exit count from the other side: NESO's own count of connection agreements it terminated, month by month.*

---

To: NESO Information Rights (box.EIR@neso.energy, or the current address on neso.energy/freedom-information)

Subject: Environmental Information Regulations request: monthly count of terminated transmission connection agreements and cancellation charges issued, from 1 October 2023 to the date of response

Dear Information Rights team,

Under the Environmental Information Regulations 2004 I request the following information, which I believe NESO holds as the counterparty to CUSC Bilateral Connection Agreements, Bilateral Embedded Generation Agreements and Construction Agreements.

1. For each calendar month from October 2023 (the month CMP376's milestone terms came into effect) to the most recent complete month before your response, the number of transmission connection agreements for generation and storage projects that were terminated in that month, split where you hold the split by (a) the reason class: User notice of termination, termination by NESO for failure of a CMP376 Queue Management milestone, and other; (b) plant type as the TEC Register prints it (Energy Storage System alone, Energy Storage System with other technologies, other); and (c) Project Status at the time of termination as the TEC Register prints it.

2. For the same months, the number of Cancellation Charge invoices issued under CUSC Section 15 and the total Cancellation Charge invoiced (GBP, excluding VAT), split where held into the Attributable Works Cancellation Charge and the Wider Cancellation Charge.

3. For the same months, the number of projects whose Transmission Entry Capacity was reduced to zero or whose agreement was otherwise ended without a termination (for example by novation into another agreement or by a technology reclassification), if you record these separately.

I ask for counts and totals only, not project names, so that no commercially confidential information about any one User is engaged. If a month holds fewer than five events in a cell, a suppressed cell ("fewer than five") is acceptable.

Please supply the information as a CSV or spreadsheet. If any part is not held, please say so for that part and supply the rest. If you consider any exception applies, please say which and apply the public-interest test; the information concerns the administration of a regulated code and the aggregate figures Ofgem's CMP470 Initial Impact Assessment (17 September 2026, paragraph 2.8) records NESO as having already provided to Ofgem.

Yours faithfully,

[name, organisation, contact details]

---

*What the answer checks.* 025 counts a project as having left the TEC Register when its chain of register rows is last printed at least 95 days before the series end and its status was not Built. NESO's monthly termination count is the same event seen from the counterparty's side. A month-by-month comparison from October 2023 shows how much of 025's exit series is termination and how much is novation, reclassification or something the register alone cannot name. The reason-class split (User notice against NESO termination for a missed milestone) is the one thing that separates a developer leaving from a developer being removed, which 025 does not claim to see.
