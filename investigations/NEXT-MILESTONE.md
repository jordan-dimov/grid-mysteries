# The next milestone (sponsor, 2026-08-27)

Grid Mysteries can now find interesting, defensible truths: reconstruct
history that public sources overwrite; join datasets never designed to be
joined; pre-declare what would matter; kill attractive stories that fail;
occasionally surface a relationship that changes how an asset should be
viewed. That question is settled.

**The next challenge is proving one of those truths is worth money in a
real capital decision.** After 009 and the September tests, no new
investigation is opened by default. The milestone to watch for:

> A fact about a specific asset, project or portfolio that an investor
> could not easily get from its normal technical adviser — and that could
> plausibly change the price or terms of the deal.

The proposition under the roof: *we test whether the economic assumptions
embedded in an energy asset survive contact with the underlying grid
evidence* — connection date, executable market access, queue position,
constraint exposure, development reality, buyer reality. The asymmetry
sought is a 2–3 % valuation change on a £50m–£500m decision, not a hidden
£1bn inefficiency.
