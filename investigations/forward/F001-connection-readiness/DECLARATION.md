# Forward Mystery F001 — what does allocating grid capacity by *evidence* create?

## Status

**Declared, public-as-of 2026-08-22.** This file is frozen at that date
and is never edited afterwards. Results, scenarios, market evidence and
outcomes are written in sibling files; this one records only what was
knowable, assumed and predicted on the declaration date. Its SHA-256 is
the protocol digest for this study.

This is the project's first **Forward Mystery**. Forensic Mysteries ask
what caused an event that already happened; this asks what a change
already under way makes hard to avoid.

## The question

Britain is changing how scarce grid capacity is allocated: away from
queue position, toward **evidence of credible project progression**.

> **What new verification, evidence-management, financing and
> transaction problems does that create — and who will be compelled to
> solve them?**

Deliberately *not* the question "are data centres booming, and will grid
connections be valuable?" Everyone knows that; it predicts nothing.

## What is publicly established as of 2026-08-22

Pinned in `evidence/` with digests. Primary sources only:

- **Ofgem, 29 July 2026** (press release and consultation "Proposed data
  centre connection reforms"): contracted demand offers rose **from
  41 GW to 125 GW between November 2024 and June 2025**; Ofgem
  attributes **at least 80 GW** of that increase to data centres, with
  ~73 GW referenced in the consultation itself. Ofgem proposes that
  developers evidence **financial capability, commercial maturity and
  procurement activity** to retain a queue position under queue
  management milestones. **The consultation closes 16 September 2026.**
- **NESO connections reform**: the queue has been reordered around
  project readiness and strategic need.

Precision the summaries blur, and which this study will not repeat: the
41→125 GW growth is measured **November 2024 to June 2025**, not "the
past year" relative to the declaration date.

## Why this study, and why now

The rule system is **being formed as this is declared** — the
consultation is open and closes 25 days from now. That is a rare chance
to develop the forward method against something happening in front of
us, rather than a historical case whose answer is already known. It also
means the study has a hard, dated falsification point: what Ofgem
actually decides.

## The five layers this study must earn, in order

No layer may be assumed from the one before it.

1. **The change** — hard evidence that something consequential is
   happening. Applications, technologies, geographies, rules. Numbers
   from primary sources with their measurement periods stated.
2. **The constraint** — what stops the system simply absorbing it.
   Candidates to test, not to assert: transmission capacity, long-lead
   electrical equipment, transformers and switchgear, planning, water,
   engineering labour, connection rights, collateral, network studies,
   regulation, balancing capability.
3. **The unavoidable problem** — derived, not guessed: *if these
   projects proceed, somebody must perform / acquire / prove / finance
   Y*. Tested under multiple scenarios, including ones where it does not
   arise.
4. **The recognition gap** — whether economic actors are **already
   behaving** as though Y matters. Mentions are not behaviour. Evidence
   corpus: procurement, job advertisements, vendor landscape,
   investment, filings, consultation responses, product offerings.
5. **The capture** — only if layers 1–4 hold. Three separate
   propositions, each earned independently: **the problem is coming**;
   **a buyer is compelled to solve it**; **existing solutions are
   inadequate**. The third is the hardest and is never inferred from the
   first two.

**If layer 5 is not earned, the honest output is a described problem and
an explicit statement that no opportunity was demonstrated.** That is a
successful study, not a failed one.

## Declared falsifiers

Recorded now so they cannot be chosen afterwards to fit the outcome.
This study's central argument is refuted if any of these proves true:

- **F-1.** Ofgem's final decision drops or materially waters down the
  evidence-of-progression requirements, so capacity is not in fact
  allocated by demonstrated readiness.
- **F-2.** Developers can produce credible readiness evidence
  internally, cheaply, with no third party needing to verify it.
- **F-3.** Counterparties who matter — NESO, financiers, offtakers —
  do not value independent verification over self-declaration.
- **F-4.** The applications driving the growth substantially withdraw,
  so the compelled population is far smaller than the headline GW.
- **F-5.** An adequate solution already exists and is already being
  bought by the identified buyers.

**F-5 is the falsifier most likely to fire**, and layer 4 exists to test
it honestly rather than to be talked past.

## Reflexivity, declared

If this study is published and is any good, it may itself influence the
behaviour it predicts. Any observed post-publication uptake is therefore
**not** confirmation. Confirming evidence must predate publication or be
independent of it, and outcome notes will say which.

## Method rules, binding

- Primary sources over reporting; a secondary claim is a **hypothesis
  lead**, never evidence, and is labelled as such. (Established the hard
  way in 003 and 004.)
- Three-column discipline throughout: **observed / supported inference /
  not publicly determinable**.
- Every quantity carries its measurement period and publisher.
- No single opportunity score. A profile across dimensions, with the
  branch of greatest information value named explicitly.
- No company is characterised as failing, naive or about to be
  disrupted. Mechanism, never accusation — as in the forensic track.

## Governed record

The v2 vocabulary was built for forensic work and does not yet have
terms for constraint, scenario, consequence, recognition or capture. It
is **deliberately not amended in advance**: the ontology emerges from a
real investigation, which is how v1 became v2. F001 will run in the repo
with this frozen declaration and its digest; whatever vocabulary it
forces will be proposed afterwards, with F001 as the forcing evidence.

The human gate for this track is **publication**, not acquisition: a
prediction cannot be un-predicted, and the falsifiers above must be
public with the claim.

## Amendment protocol

Amendments are legitimate **before layer-1 evidence is analysed**,
recorded with honest chronology in the results file, and never after.
This declaration file itself is never edited.
