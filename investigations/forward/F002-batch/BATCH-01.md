## Frozen kill order

Kills run in **ascending cost of the cheap forcing observation** — the
research-leverage principle applied to the batch, not to taste — with
one binding exception stated below:

**C5 → C1 → C6 → C7 → C8 → C3 → C10 → C9 → C4 → C2**

**C2 runs last, and its result is quarantined.** C2 is adjacent to F001,
whose evidence corpus this project has already mined. A strain finding
there would be contaminated by prior exposure and **may not count as
independent evidence about Generator v3's quality**. It is carried
because deleting it would be a taste decision; it is quarantined because
counting it would be re-mining.

## Kill results are four-valued

`absorption stable` · `absorption strain evidenced` ·
`absorption strain plausible but not measured` ·
`absorption not determinable`

A candidate reaching *plausible* must have its cheap forcing observation
run before any deeper work. If the predicted symptom is absent, it is
killed or downgraded — it does not sit in "plausible" accumulating
research.

## Scoring commitment, made before any result exists

The generator is scored on all ten. Declared in advance so the result
cannot be reinterpreted afterwards:

- **≤2 of 10 showing evidenced or plausible strain** → Generator v3 is
  weak, even if one candidate later becomes a good business.
- **3–4 of 10** → the generator is doing something, weakly.
- **≥5 of 10** → the generator locates economic systems under adaptation
  pressure, which is the thing actually being detected — even if none of
  the five becomes a standalone business.
- **Many `not determinable`** → the failure is the *forcing observations*
  in this file, not the candidates, and the next revision is to the
  observation design.

Where the value from adaptation pressure accrues — software, specialists,
incumbents, labour, assets, standards, suppliers or investors — is a
later question. **This batch does not assume it accrues to a startup.**
