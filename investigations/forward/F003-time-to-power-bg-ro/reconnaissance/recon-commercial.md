# Commercial-falsification reconnaissance: "powered land" sponsor thesis, Bulgaria and Romania

Reconnaissance date: 2026-08-25. Languages searched: English, Bulgarian, Romanian.
Produced by a background research agent; its working file was lost in a session
restart and this copy is transcribed from the agent's returned report. Content is
otherwise unaltered.

Verification legend: **[V]** page fetched and read; **[L]** primary document
downloaded and text-extracted locally; **[S]** snippet-only (search summary; page
not opened due to paywall/403/503/budget); **[N]** searched for and not found /
could not verify.

Thesis under test: *a sponsor uses public grid data to find BG/RO transmission
nodes where a new 50–150 MW load connects unusually fast/cheaply, secures land +
grid path + fibre + planning, and sells/JVs the "powered land" to a data-centre
developer before institutional capital arrives; the edge is seeing valuable MW
before conventional land developers do.*

## 0. Summary (observation → interpretation → conclusion)

**Observations**

1. Bulgaria's TSO already publishes a public per-substation "free capacity" map
   that takes *consumption* as an input type and returns voltage level, an
   indicative connection price and, where relevant, the overloaded lines and
   their reconstruction cost; updated at least quarterly. [L][V]
2. As of 3 Aug 2026 ESO had 9,330 MW of data-centre connection
   requests/intentions, 3,795 MW already with ESO opinions; opinions valid one
   year; ESO does not sign preliminary contracts with these applicants. [V]
   May 2026: ~9,000 MW / 3,710 MW with opinions. [V]
3. BG study fee for a >100 MVA consumer: 7,410 BGN (€3,788.67) excl. VAT;
   Naredba 6 (to 14 Apr 2026) imposes **no financial guarantee at opinion stage
   for a new consumption object** (the new €25,564.59/MW guarantee applies only
   to storage added at existing consumption sites). [V][L]
4. Romania's DEN head (15 Mar 2026): a serious DC investor with a competent
   consultant "will have the ATR from us in under three months". [V]
   Transelectrica solution-study cost: 119,000 lei (50–150 MW), 144,000 lei
   (≥150 MW), excl. VAT. [L]
5. Romania's TSO map and July-2026 allocation auctions cover *production* only;
   no public consumer hosting-capacity map found. [V]
6. The connection position attaches to the site/SPV in both countries: BG
   Naredba 6 Art. 20(2); RO Ordin 59/2013 Art. 25(3)(b). [L]
7. Live instances of the thesis exist: Resita Data Infrastructure SRL (RO, Apr
   2026, 3 ha next to a 400/220/110 kV substation commissioned Nov 2024,
   solution study registered 29 May 2026, selling majority of SPV) [V];
   EuRODataPark (23 ha, 38 MVA) [V]; Power Loop/AG Capital (no sites disclosed)
   [V]; Trakia Economic Zone's "Stryama" park allocating 245 dka to Brinell [V].
8. No CEE powered-land transaction with a disclosed €/MW was found. Only
   reference: JLL FLAP-D prime powered land €2.26m/MW (mid-2026),
   secondary/tertiary discounts 2.3x/4x. [V]
9. Realised BG demand is thin (colocation ~2 MW halls; three MoUs with
   undisclosed MW); RO more real (ClusterPower 10 MW live, 70 MW by end-2026;
   Portland Trust designing a 2×50 MVA station). [V][S]

**Interpretation.** In Bulgaria the informational edge is public, cheap to act
on, and already acted on at ~9 GW, mostly by parties ESO and industry call
speculative; the scarce good is credibility with ESO and a firm instrument,
which does not exist at opinion stage. In Romania the information is not public
but is purchasable from the TSO for ~€25–30k in ~3 months, and the TSO actively
courts large loads. Legal mechanics for selling the position work in both
countries. Value concentrates at firm milestones (RO: ATR + contract +
guarantee; BG: contract after building permit), which need permits, land
ownership and cash — conventional development capital.

**Conclusion (narrow).** "Seeing valuable MW first" is falsified for Bulgaria
and weak for Romania. "Selling a connection position" is not falsified. Whether
a buyer pays a material premium for a BG opinion or RO ATR at 50–150 MW is
unproven: no CEE price point, small realised demand. The surviving thesis is a
conventional pre-development play (land + permits + firm connection) in
Romania, competing with at least three named sponsors, two utilities and the
TSO's own promotional posture.

## 4. Connection process for large NEW consumers

### 4.1 Bulgaria — Наредба № 6/28.03.2024 (КЕВР), consolidated to ДВ 35/14.04.2026 [L]

- **Steps (Art. 2(3)):** искане → становище → предварителен договор (before
  investment design) → договор за присъединяване (after approved design +
  effective building permit, Art. 2(6)).
- **Who may apply (Art. 7(2)(1)):** new object requires "вещно право да строи в
  имота". Art. 2(4): document of real right to build, or [2024] notarised
  preliminary sale/building-right contract or municipal/forestry decision "в
  случаите на … ВИ", or notarised consent/lease. **Ambiguity preserved:**
  unclear whether a notarised preliminary purchase contract suffices for a
  *consumer* study.
- **Study fee (ESO price list, Ц-27/2016, Ц-24/2023) [V]:** ≤15 MVA 4,800 BGN;
  15–100 MVA 5,480 BGN (€2,801.88); >100 MVA 7,410 BGN (€3,788.67), excl. VAT.
- **Opinion deadline (Art. 15(2)(1)):** 14 days; extendable by agreement for
  complex schemes.
- **Opinion validity (Art. 16(3)):** one year. ESO confirms one-year opinions,
  no preliminary contracts for DC applicants. [V]
- **Guarantee (Art. 16а, amended 14.04.2026):** €25,564.59/MW within 3 months —
  only for Art. 7(1) items 15 and 17 (storage at existing consumption sites).
  **None for new consumption objects.**
- **Preliminary contract (Art. 18):** draft in 15 days; validity 2 years.
- **Connection contract (Art. 20–22):** needs effective building permit +
  approved design (Art. 21(2)); lapse → new request (Art. 22(3)).
- **Transfer (Art. 20(2)):** contract "може да се сключи и с лице, което е
  титуляр на право на собственост или друго вещно или облигационно право за
  ползване на обекта, за който друго лице има издадено становище … или има
  сключен предварителен договор"; Art. 26(1)(18) contract clause on party
  substitution. Position runs with property/SPV.
- **Reinforcement cost:** Art. 12(3)(4) price/estimate in opinion; Art. 28(5)
  network elements at operator cost unless individual-project pricing (ЗЕ Art.
  36(3)); **Art. 26(1)(19)** applicant may assume full cost of
  transfer-capability works not in the operator's programme, with later
  pro-rata reimbursement. ESO map: investor exceeding free capacity chooses to
  pay for reinforcing overloaded elements or reduce power; switchgear
  extensions and land "за сметка на инвеститора". [L]
- **Temporary access (Art. 23):** public register of requests in order of
  receipt; first-in-line served (23(5)).
- **Refusal (Art. 14):** technical impossibility by requested date, or
  deterioration for others "поради липса … на необходимата мощност".
- **Is the study the only way to learn headroom? No.** ESO map: pick
  substation, "Производител/Потребител", MW → voltage level, approximate
  connection value, and for some substations "застрашените от претоварване
  електропроводи … и приблизителни разходи за тяхната реконструкция";
  "актуализират веднъж на тримесечие или по-често"; explicitly not a
  reservation or commitment; ignores simultaneous applicants/rings. [L]
- **Public register of consumer applications:** not found (ESO register covers
  RES producers). [S][N]
- **Speculation controls:** none enacted for consumers; ESO CEO seeks
  legislative change to refuse speculative/prioritise strategic projects,
  opposed by industry (24may.bg opinion piece [V]); Noveltel CEO proposes
  two-substation requirement and credential checks [S]. No draft ЗЕ amendment
  found. [N]

### 4.2 Romania — Ordin ANRE 59/2013 + amendments; Legea 123/2012

- **Threshold:** >50 MVA requests go to the TSO (TEL guide §6, 2011). [L]
- **Steps:** cerere → study offer (5 wd) → studiu de soluție (≤3 months; Ordin
  20/2025 Art. 18(4)) → aviz CTES (60 days, 2011 guide) → ATR (10 days, Ordin
  20/2025 Art. 26(3)) → contract draft (10 days, Art. 37) → certificat de
  racordare. [L][V]
- **Study cost (TEL Anexa 2) [L]:** 119,000 lei (50–150 MW), 144,000 lei
  (≥150 MW), excl. VAT; ATR tariffs 270/350/435 lei.
- **ATR documents [L]:** certificat de urbanism, stereo-70 site plan, act de
  proprietate or right of use (notarised owner consent if leased), avize de
  amplasament, technical data (Ordin 51/2009); land-right document valid to
  year-end (Ordin 53/2024 [S]).
- **ATR validity (Art. 32–33) [L]:** until certificate, but ceases (b) "în
  termen de 12 luni de la emitere, dacă nu a fost încheiat contractul de
  racordare"; (a) 3 months to post guarantee for producers >1 MW; (d)/(e) with
  underlying permits. Ordin 20/2025 Art. 34(1): request contract ≥45 days
  before expiry. [V]
- **Permit milestone (Art. 36(5)/(5¹)/(6)):** building permit within 12 months
  of contract / 18 months of ATR, else ATR ceases and contract terminates "de
  drept". Text names "locul de producere sau … locul de consum şi de producere"
  — **pure consumption sites not explicit**; flag.
- **Guarantees [L]:** Art. 31(3) 5% of tariff (pre-2026). **Ordin 15/2026 (MO
  436/25.05.2026):** 20% of tariff excl. VAT for "putere aprobată pentru
  evacuare mai mare de 1 MW"; ANRE establishment authorisation for
  production/storage >1 MW; +5% per 12-month extension; allocation
  participation guarantee €20,000/MW (2026); Grigorescu cites 30 EUR/kW
  authorisation guarantee. **All keyed to evacuated power; no
  consumer-specific guarantee found.** [N]
- **Transfer (Art. 25(3)(b)) [L]:** "schimbarea titularului locului de consum …
  prin cumpărare, moştenire etc." = administrative modification, no ATR update.
  No change-of-control clause for SPV share sales found. [N]
- **Reinforcement (Art. 36(5⁴)–(5⁶)) [L]:** first user pays întărire, later
  users reimburse pro rata; ATR states pre-pay option.
- **Non-public information? Partly.** Art. 67: documentation underlying the
  solution available on request; "Datele tehnice … au caracter de
  confidenţialitate." TEL map (harti_crd_tel) is production-only, 10 zones,
  2025/2030, N-1, Ordin 137/2021; allocation procedure/auctions "pentru …
  locurilor de producere". [V] For a consumer the study is the formal route —
  sold by the TSO in ≤3 months for ~€24–29k.
- **TSO posture (15 Mar 2026, V. Ivan, DEN):** "În mai puţin de trei luni, dacă
  au un consultant serios … vor avea ATR-ul"; one-stop office under PM. [V]
- **Speculation controls:** ATR-holder list published 25 Apr 2026 (80 GW);
  ENTSO-E cites Romania among allocation reformers. [V][L]
- **Vintage caveat:** TEL guide is 2011 (ATR 6+6 months) — superseded; used
  only for steps and the 50 MVA threshold.

## 5. Local-knowledge cost

- **BG:** free (ESO map). ELFOR still markets "получаване на информация за
  възможността за присъединяване към електропреносната мрежа, получаване на
  становище от ЕСО, изготвяне на предварителен и окончателен договор" [S, 500];
  New Balkans Law Office guide [S].
- **RO:** studiu de soluție providers are a regulated commodity (ANRE
  Regulament 2015 [S]); CIGA Energy offers connection-permit documentation and
  studies for large consumers, no DC mention [V]; Electritec, Electro Calin,
  BMA [S]. DEN head's "un consultant serios" presupposes this market.
- **Conclusion:** marginal cost of headroom knowledge ≈ €0 (BG) or ~€25–40k +
  3 months (RO). No evidence of privileged ex-TSO knowledge being sold; none
  needed.

## 7. Other kills

- **Public hosting-capacity data:** BG map now; Capacitypedia (22 May 2026, 27
  countries) aggregates national publications [S]; ENTSO-E (30 Apr 2026)
  recommends publication of "where grid capacity is available, what connection
  timelines look like" and warns "speculative requests may concentrate in areas
  with available capacity" without queue management (guarantees, maturity
  milestones incl. land rights/permits, withdrawal penalties, capacity release)
  [L]; Commission guidance C/2025/8473 recommends "first-ready first-served" and
  "transparent maturity criteria" [V, EC overview]; the "TSO monthly / DSO
  quarterly" update rule appeared only in a search summary and could not be
  traced to legal text [S].
- **RO has no consumer map; reforms target producers first.** [L][N]
- **TSO plans:** ESO 2025–2034 plan (40 pp) contains **no mention of data
  centres**; 22,743 MW new generation, 41,600 GWh by 2034; works for RES and
  coal exit. [L] The plan does not flag DC nodes — the map does.
- **Grid physics:** ESO CEO: 9 GW would need Kozloduy 7/8 and Belene; BG may
  become net importer; thermal 37–42% of winter supply [V]; Noveltel CEO: even
  4 GW "unrealistic for Southeast Europe" [S].
- **Fibre:** not binding — BDGW routes (Burgas–Alexandroupolis,
  Sofia–Thessaloniki, Ruse–Bucharest), €12.8m CEF to 10 BG organisations 2025,
  Kardesa 2027, RETN; Resita/EuRODataPark claim 2–4 carriers. [S][V]
- **Planning:** BG contract needs effective building permit (Art. 21); RO ATR
  needs CU + permit milestones; industrial-park operators (TEZ) deliver
  permitted land faster than a lone sponsor. [L][V]
- **Water:** no BG/RO evidence; Resita cites Bârzava 3.63 m³/s. [N]
- **Utility/state competition:** DEER, Transelectrica, PPC (RO); Kozloduy/Belene
  and government MoUs (BG). [V]
