# F004 — the stranded Bulgarian data-centre pipeline: absorption kill, declared before it is run

**Declared 2026-08-26. Frozen on commit; never edited afterwards.** Results go
in `KILL-RESULT.md`. This is a single commissioned candidate, not a Generator
v3 batch; it is run under the v3 kill discipline
(`../CANDIDATE-PROTOCOL.md`) so that the same four-valued verdict and the same
prohibition on promotion apply.

## The candidate function

> **Converting a speculative Bulgarian data-centre connection position —
> an ESO opinion, some land control, partial permitting — into a permitted,
> financed, buyer-backed project; and, upstream of that, sorting which of
> many such positions are worth converting.**

Commercial form proposed by the reviewer: acquire or JV the best stranded
positions cheaply, supply development capital and credibility, package to
institutional standard, sell to global data-centre capital. Grid Mysteries'
role would be diligence and selection, not the product.

## Evidence used only to justify generation

Known before this file was written; none of it is evidence about whether the
function is absorbed.

- ESO's public map exposes per-node capacity by queue stage (F003, pinned).
- ESO figures, 3 Aug 2026: 9,330 MW of data-centre applications and
  intentions; 5.535 GW still intentions; the rest with issued opinions; ESO
  says realisation within the one-year validity "depends on the behaviour of
  the investors themselves" (F003 manifest, economic.bg 2026-08-12).
- Naredba 6/2024: opinion in 14 days, €2.8–3.8k, one-year validity, no
  exclusivity, no guarantee for a new consumption site; preliminary contract
  requires a right to build; connection contract requires an effective
  building permit; the position may pass to a person holding rights over the
  site (Art. 20(2)).
- Secondary, unpinned: applicants described in press as "€1-capital
  companies, oilseed processors, RES/BESS developers"; ESO's CEO seeking
  legal power to refuse speculative projects; three government MoUs with
  undisclosed MW; realised Bulgarian data-centre demand at single-digit MW
  per facility.
- Romania, as the thesis in miniature: Resita Data Infrastructure SRL markets
  a 3 ha site beside a new 400/220/110 kV substation with a registered
  solution study and no ATR, seeking an SPV buyer or JV; **no buyer
  announced** (F003 commercial reconnaissance).

## Who already gets paid to perform this function

The absorption question, asked first:

1. **The promoters themselves.** Many applicants are RES/BESS developers
   whose trade is converting grid positions into financed projects. If they
   convert at a healthy rate, nothing is stranded.
2. **Powered-land developers and industrial-zone operators.** Power Loop
   (AG Capital), Trakia Economic Zone and peers — their stated business is
   exactly land + grid + permits packaged for a buyer.
3. **Data-centre developers' and hyperscalers' own site-selection teams**,
   which buy directly from promoters without an intermediary.
4. **Real-estate distress and infrastructure investors**, for whom "good
   asset, weak sponsor" is the ordinary case.
5. **Local law and consulting firms** (ELFOR and peers) that package
   applications, opinions and contracts as a service.

Two standing cautions, recorded so they cannot be talked past:

- **The opinion is worth approximately nothing by construction** — cheap,
  non-exclusive, one year, no reservation. Whatever value a stranded position
  has is land, permits and local process. That is real-estate development,
  and the null hypothesis is that (2) and (4) absorb it without strain.
- **The fact that strands projects is the fact that makes them worthless.**
  Positions strand because buyers are few. A conversion business needs the
  buyer proposition earned *first*; F001's rule that no opportunity may be
  named before all three propositions are earned applies with full force,
  and the buyer proposition is the one reconnaissance found weakest
  (realised demand in Bulgaria at MW scale; no CEE transaction price found).

## Predicted symptoms if absorption is under strain

If the function is genuinely not being absorbed, at least one of these
should already be observable:

- **S1 — positions offered without takers.** Sites "with ESO opinion for a
  data centre" marketed for sale or JV (brokers, listings, LinkedIn, press
  "seeking partner"), persisting without a transaction.
- **S2 — lapse without conversion.** On the ESO map, opinion-stage MW
  disappearing at or after its one-year mark without appearing at
  preliminary-contract stage, at a rate materially above conversion.
- **S3 — institutional strain.** ESO or KEVR changing the process for
  consumers (guarantees, refusal powers, maturity criteria) because the
  flood cannot be handled by the existing rules.
- **S4 — concentration of MW in entities without capital.** A large share
  of opinion-stage MW held by SPVs with nominal capital and no disclosed
  partner.
- **S5 — price discovery.** Any Bulgarian transaction of land + opinion, at
  any price, or a documented €/MW.

Eligibility (L1): S1, S2 and S5 can contain the failure by construction;
S3 is institutional strain, which is *TSO-side* strain and is not by itself
evidence that the sponsor function is unabsorbed (L9 caution: process
adaptation is weak evidence of a capacity ceiling); S4 shows that promoters
are weak, not that nobody absorbs their positions.

## Pre-declared readings

- O1 finds no marketed positions **and** O2/O4 show opinions converting to
  preliminary contracts at a healthy rate → **absorption stable** (the
  promoters convert themselves). Stop.
- O1 finds a visible secondary market with asking prices → the function is
  already externalising. Apply the F001 chain — *ProvisionExists ≠
  ProvisionPurchased ≠ AdequateFor ≠ MarketSaturated* — and look for a
  transaction. A transaction → **efficiently served** or **category
  emergence**, decided by penetration and repeat purchase, not by count.
- O2 shows mass lapse, O1 shows no market, S5 finds no price → positions are
  stranding **and nobody wants them**. That is a **buyer-side need failure**,
  recorded as "described problem, no opportunity", not as an acquisition
  thesis.
- Only **lapse + marketed positions + at least one buyer paying at ≥50 MW in
  Bulgaria** (t_public ≤ the date of the reading) reaches **absorption strain
  evidenced**, and even then propositions 2 and 3 (compelled buyer; existing
  solutions inadequate) remain to be earned separately.
- Data exists operationally at ESO but is not published and O4 is refused →
  **plausible but not measured**, downgraded per L7, with O4 recorded as the
  observation that would resolve it.

## Declared falsifiers of the commercial form

- **F-A.** No data-centre developer or hyperscaler has acquired or
  contracted a Bulgarian site at ≥50 MW by the date of the reading — the
  buyer does not yet exist in this market.
- **F-B.** Marketed positions transact, with promoters selling directly to
  developers — the intermediary is not needed.
- **F-C.** Powered-land incumbents already hold the positions that survive
  diligence — the sponsor role is taken.
- **F-D.** Conversion rates are healthy — nothing strands.

## Reflexivity

Publishing that Bulgarian positions are stranding and for sale could itself
create the market it describes. Any post-publication uptake is not
confirmation; confirming evidence must predate publication.

## Not in scope

Contacting promoters, valuing specific projects, or originating transactions.
Those are actions for a sponsor, gated by a human decision, and they begin
only if this kill returns strain evidenced.
