## What was asked, restated

Not "will many projects fail?" — they will — but: *are good projects failing
for a remediable reason, while buyers demonstrably value the remediated
state?* The declaration pre-committed that "lots of weak projects" is
ambiguous, not bullish, and that the buyer proposition must be earned first.

## Observations (O1, O3; full records in `O1-MARKETED-POSITIONS.md`,
`O3-POPULATION-RECONSTRUCTION.md`)

**S1 — positions offered without takers.** No Bulgarian site or SPV is
publicly marketed as "data centre + ESO opinion for X MW". Two sites carry
data-centre framing without an opinion (a 135 dka plot near Varna at
€35/m²; "Project Provadia", a 100 MW PV site pitched as "AI-ready"); one
chamber-of-commerce note records an unnamed investor's interest in a former
industrial site beside an ESO substation. Brokers, portals, LinkedIn, zone
operators, Power Loop: nothing. The Romanian comparator (Resita, study
registered 29 May 2026, terms "under NDA") has announced no buyer since
April.

But the counterparty describes the *intent* to sell in its own words. ESO's
chief executive, Capital, 19 Aug 2026 (pinned body,
`data/raw/press/capital-bg-tsunami-data-centres_2026-08-19.txt`):

> „Българските инвеститори искат първо да си осигурят достъп до
> електропреносната мрежа, тъй като получаването на положително становище
> увеличава стойността на проекта и позволява той да бъде продаден на
> стратегически инвеститор."

and: "Ако един инвеститор резервира 300 MW днес, конкурентът му може след
година вече да няма откъде да се присъедини. Това създава стимул
заявленията да бъдат за по-големи мощности от реално необходимите."

**Population coverage.** ESO: "всички заявки идват от български компании, но
имената им не са публични". No register of consumer requests or opinions
exists. O3 found **zero of 3,795 MW** attributable to a named opinion holder,
and ≥85 % of the 9,330 MW untraceable by any public trace — municipal ПУП
decisions, RIOSV/MOEW EIA notices, BAI certificates, KEVR, parliament, press

opinion and no land purchase between them**; Brinell — the flagship, €3 bn,

customer, financing or contractor as of 31 Jul 2026.

**The one fully identified "developer re-purposes a site" case** — Campus
Tenevo (Renalfa, beside its 238 MW PV / 315 MW BESS park) — stranded on
**19 June 2026 when Tundzha municipal council unanimously revoked its land
consent after a residents' meeting**. That is a local-consent failure, not a
capital, sponsor or market-access failure; a sponsor's money does not
remediate it.

**F-A — a buyer at ≥50 MW.** Not triggered. The largest evidenced

hyperscaler land purchase; Schwarz Digits and the US company are MoUs; Meta
says "after 2028"; IBM's gigafactory has been silent since October 2025.

**S2 / S5 — lapse rate, price.** Not observable today. O2's baseline

## Reading against the pre-declared outcomes

- *Stable* requires healthy conversion (O2/O4) — not available. Not claimed.
- *Efficiently served / category emergence* requires a visible secondary
  market and a transaction — none.
- *Strain evidenced* requires lapse **and** marketed positions **and** a
  buyer paying at ≥50 MW — the buyer is absent, the market is absent, the
  lapse is unmeasured. **Not reached.**
- *Plausible but not measured* — the series that would decide (opinions
  issued / lapsed / converted, held by ESO) is not published. **This is the
  reading**, and under v3 it is **downgraded**, recorded with O4 as the
  observation that resolves it.

The commercial form fails independently of the strain question: with no
buyer at scale, the population of "stranded positions" — however large — is
supply without demand. As the review put it before the run: an expiring
€3.8k non-exclusive opinion is not distressed infrastructure; it may simply
be a lottery ticket nobody wants. On today's evidence that is the state of
the Bulgarian pipeline. **No opportunity is demonstrated, and the opposite
inference — that stranding here is evidence of absence of value rather than
mispricing — is the better-supported one.**

## What the run is entitled to keep

- **The function is being self-supplied.** ESO's own description is of
  promoters performing the "sort, package and sell to a strategic investor"
  role themselves; the missing party is the strategic investor. The kill
  found the right shape and the wrong side of the market.
- **The one stranding mechanism actually observed was local consent**, not
  capital. n = 1; recorded, not generalised.
- **O2 is the right instrument and it is already running.** The per-node
  `opinion → pd` transition is conversion; `opinion → gone` is lapse or
  withdrawal (not separable without O4). Conversion rates by node, voltage
  and MW bucket are the useful series; they remain an instrument, not a
  thesis.
- **Eligibility note (L1/L7).** Public listings are an eligible but weak
  instrument for S1: ESO's quote implies positions are marketed *privately*
  to strategic investors under NDA (Resita's terms are explicitly so). Their
  absence from public channels is therefore not strong evidence of absence;
  what is strong is the absence of any transaction at scale, which would be
  public.
