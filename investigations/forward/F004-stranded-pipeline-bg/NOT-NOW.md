# Bulgarian powered-land / stranded-pipeline — NOT NOW; reopen only on buyer evidence

**Decided 2026-08-26**, after F003 (plan) and F004 (kill), by the sponsor.

## What is parked

The whole family of Bulgarian data-centre powered-land ideas: discovering
hidden capacity (F003-1, absorbed — ESO publishes it), acquiring or JV-ing
stranded positions (F004, killed by F-A — no buyer), and any adjacent
variant that starts from the 9.3 GW pipeline. The pipeline is read as
**speculative inventory on one side of a trade whose other side has not
appeared**: promoters, by ESO's own account, obtain opinions in order to sell
to strategic investors; no strategic investor has bought at scale.

## Reopening triggers — any one, evidenced with t_public and a primary source

1. An acquisition or JV of a Bulgarian development site at **≥50 MW**
   (land + grid instrument), with the buyer named.
2. A **binding** capacity agreement between a hyperscaler or operator and a
   Bulgarian site.
3. Disclosed project financing or final investment decision for one of the
   announced mega-projects (Brinell, Schwarz Digits, the unnamed US company,
   IBM, Meta/Kozloduy).
4. Several opinion-stage projects visibly progressing through ПУП / ОВОС /
   building permit into credible construction — observable in O2's
   `opinion → pd → contract` transitions and in municipal and RIOSV records.
5. Evidence that strategic buyers are actively **bidding** for partially
   developed positions (a transaction, a mandate, or a broker process with a
   named buyer — not a promoter's claim).

Until one fires, no new Bulgarian fact — however exciting — reopens the
question. A trigger firing reopens the *question*, not the thesis: it sends
the candidate back through a fresh absorption kill.
