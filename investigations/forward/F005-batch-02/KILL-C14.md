### (b) FES revision width (proxy only)

[V] "Changes from FES 2024 to FES 2025": data centres 2050 54–62 TWh →
51–71 TWh (range width 8 → 20 TWh); I&C excl. electrolysis 2050 212–276 →
262–329 TWh (level up ~50 TWh); "Out to 2030 electricity demand increases
at a faster rate than it did in FES 2024"; uncertainty "captured by a range
in … the level of data centre demand growth". "Changes from FES 2023 to
FES 2024": data-centre growth increased; overall ranges *narrowing* under
the pathway framework. So the data-centre range narrowed then widened.
NESO's caveats (FES 2025): current fleet "7.6 TWh from the 2.4 GW
connected facilities"; "The main uncertainty for future data centre demand
is how much will be located within Great Britain"; stakeholders said
"ramp-up rates were too conservative", so NESO raised them; "High
uncertainty on future growth of data centres". No series of demand-
connection pipeline *conversion* revisions exists [N].

**Verdict (b): measured (proxy) — data-centre range doubled in width FES
2024→2025 after narrowing 2023→2024; a scenario-range signal, not a
conversion series.**
