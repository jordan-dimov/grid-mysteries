## Answer 1 — current absorber

- Ofgem, 24 Aug 2022, §2.3–2.4 [V]: "there is some work that must be
  undertaken by a DNO … 'non-contestable' … there are some connection
  activities where competition has the potential to exist … work can be
  undertaken by parties other than the DNO, such as Independent Connection
  Providers ('ICPs') and licensed Independent Distribution Network
  Operators ('IDNOs')."
- Ofgem CoP decision, 30 Jun 2015 [V]: "only National Electricity
  Registration Scheme (NERS) accreditations are required … administered by
  Lloyd's Register"; licence condition "legally binding effect from mid
  October 2015".
- NERS Requirements Document v10 (Mar 2025) [V]: EHV "greater than 20kV up
  to and including 132kV"; construction scopes "awarded in defined voltage
  ratings (e.g. 11kV, 20kV, 33kV 66kV or 132kV)"; design scopes include
  "Self-determination of Point of Connection (POC) at EHV" ("NOs may
  operate pilots limited to specific voltages").
- CoP allocation (Ofgem Oct 2014 annex) [V]: **non-contestable** — POC
  determination; upstream reinforcement design; design approval; third-
  party wayleaves; live jointing to the distribution system; reinforcement
  on the existing system; QA inspections; commissioning. **Contestable** —
  extension-asset design; diversion design; procurement; land-rights
  negotiation; trenching/ducting; substation civils; cable laying; plant
  installation; jointing bays; dead jointing; live LV jointing on newly
  adopted assets; testing. CoP mods 0001 (POC self-determination) and
  0002 (design self-approval) required "by no later than 15 January 2016".
  2025 register: POC self-determination held at LV by 103 providers, 11 kV
  65, 33 kV 39, 66 kV 30, 132 kV 31 [V].
- Governance Document V1.2 §3.24 [V]: a "Convertible Quotation …
  separately identifies the charges for Non-Contestable Works and
  Contestable Works".

## Answer 2 — absorption status

**ADEQUATE — ALREADY EXTERNALISED (mature since 2015; 33/132 kV ICP
capacity more than doubled).**

**(i) Market age** [V]: competition in electricity connections dates from
2000; Ofgem 2014: "the development of competition for electricity
connections had been slow … 87% of metered electricity connections … were
completed by the incumbent"; DPCR5 Competition Test 2012–14; CoP
enforceable mid-Oct 2015. Ofgem 2022, Table 8: of 84 relevant market
segments, 24 passed the competition test for ED2; Metered Demand HV&EHV
and EHV+ passed only in SSES; DG HV&EHV passed in seven areas; several EHV
demand segments "out of scope" / "no data provided". Interpretation [S]:
Ofgem's test measures whether *competition* is effective, not delivery
capacity.

**(ii) Volume/share** [V, none current]: ICPs "provided 14% (by volume) of
connections to DNO networks in 2010-11 compared to 32% in 2013-14" (Ofgem
2014); NPg metered demand HV "77% … by value … completed by competitors
between 2010 and 2012". No current GB-wide contestable share at 33/132 kV
found [N]. ENWL MCAR 2024-25: 3,466 budgets & quotes issued to ICPs/IDNOs,
471 accepted — the only ICP-application volume metric located.

**Reading.** Time-to-quote for Demand HV/EHV and EHV+ fell in three of
