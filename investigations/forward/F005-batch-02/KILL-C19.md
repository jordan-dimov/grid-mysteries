## 1. Current absorber

**(a) Operator's own appointed Authorised Persons (HV), audited by an
Authorising Engineer (HV)** — [V] HTM 06-03 *Electrical safety guidance
for high voltage systems* (NHS England, 2023; "supersedes all previous
versions"; change log headed "Main changes since the **2006 edition**").
Duty Holder → Designated Person → Authorising Engineer (HV) → Authorised
Persons (HV) → Competent Persons (HV). AP eligibility: nominated by
Management, "assessed, interviewed and recommended for appointment by the
Authorising Engineer"; qualified; AP (HV) training course; refresher "at
intervals not exceeding three years". 6.7 "The Management should designate
an appropriate room suitable for Authorised Persons to undertake their
duties" (the AP office). New in 2023: "the option to produce electronic
safety documents … No particular electronic system is endorsed or
prescribed."

**(b) Defence estate: JSP 375 Vol 3 Ch 3 "Electricity"** — [V] gov.uk
collection published 1 Nov 2009, updated 30 Nov 2020; Chapter 3 (revised

**(c) HSE's framing of the private/commercial operator** — [V] HSG230
*Keeping electrical switchgear safe* (2015): "aimed at owners and
operators of electrical switchgear in industrial and commercial
organisations"; HSG85 *Electricity at work: Safe working practices* (2013).
Both address the duty holder — HSE assumes the operator absorbs it.

**(d) Third-party contractors performing AP duties / COMA** — [V] HTM
06-03 Chapter 5 "revised to provide more guidance on situations where

5.19 "Where any third-party organisation is contracted to perform

**(e) FM contractors and specialist AE firms** — [V] HTM 06-03

## 2. Absorption status

**Adequate — already externalised, and long ago.**
- The AE/AP outsourcing pattern is not emergent: HTM 06-03 (2006 edition;
  2023 revision *adds* COMA guidance because the practice is common) and
  JSP 375 (2009) define the contract forms (COMA, demarcation agreement,
  letter of competence, transfer-of-control certificate). Eta Projects, an
  AE supplier winning three public AE contracts 2023–2026, was
  incorporated in 1996 [V]. "Outsourced AP/AE services as a standard FM
  line" is at least ~20 years old on the public side [S: inferred from
  guidance vintages].
- Forcing variable not observed in the proxy: Contracts Finder notices
  mentioning HV at all ran 70 / 87 / 77 / 32 / 23 for 2022–2026-to-date [V,
  pooled; 2025–26 partially truncated] — no rise.
- The private-side absorber (data centres, industrial) is unverified [N];
  the claim that it mirrors HTM/JSP is [S].
- The 2023 HTM's neutral stance on "electronic safety documents" is the
  only visible seam — a tooling question, not an unabsorbed function.
