## C11 — LV connection assessment & quotation at volume — CLASS C (PASS)
Buyer class: DNOs (obligated). Budget owner: each electricity distribution licensee ("electricity distributor").
Evidence:
- [V] SI 2015/698 Electricity (Connection Standards of Performance) Regulations 2015, Reg 5: applies when a customer gives s16A(1) notice for "a single LV service demand connection or small project demand connection" and pays connection offer expenses; distributor that "fails to dispatch a quotation to the customer within the prescribed period from the applicable date" must pay the prescribed sum. Schedule 1: Reg 5 = 5 working days (15 wd extended), "£15 for each working day after the end of the prescribed period"; Reg 6 (other demand) 25/35/65 wd at £65/£135/£200 per wd. https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2015/698/regulation/5/made ; .../schedule/1/made
- [V] Ofgem direction 27 Oct 2023, RIIO-ED2 RIGs Annex G – Connections v1.1, "applies from 1 October 2023", issued following "amendments to the Electricity (Connection Standards of Performance) Regulations 2015" (2023 amendments exist; not read). https://www.ofgem.gov.uk/decision/direction-issuing-update-riio-ed2-regulatory-instructions-and-guidance-annex-g-connections
- [V] Ofgem consultation 27 Feb 2026 "Changes to RIIO-ED2 load related expenditure volume drivers": LRE volume drivers = Low Voltage Services Volume Driver (LVSVD) + Secondary Reinforcement Volume Driver (SRVD), SpC 3.9; proposes "upward revision to LVSVD unit rates" and "higher LVSVD ex-ante allowance for all DNOs", effective 1 April 2026; decision issued 11 June 2026. https://www.ofgem.gov.uk/consultation/changes-riio-ed2-load-related-expenditure-volume-drivers
Recurring: YES (per-application penalty exposure + annual volume-driver allowance). Large-and-repeatable: no.
Caveats: obligation is per quotation, not "at volume"; 2015 regime predates EV/HP surge; Reg 15 exemptions; LVSVD funds LV *services* delivery, quotation labour is inside it only by inference.

Budget owner: transmission-connected demand facility owner (DCC Art 34); distribution-connected "User" whose load must comply (Distribution Code DPC4.2.3.2).

Art 34(7): "Any planned test schedules and procedures to verify compliance ... shall be notified to the relevant system operator ... and approved by the relevant system operator prior to their commencement." Art 35 relevant system operator verifies via testing/simulation/monitoring (Arts 36-47). https://www.legislation.gov.uk/eur/2016/1388/article/34 ; .../article/59

(c) Phase (Voltage) Unbalance shall comply with ... P29". https://dcode.org.uk/assets/2026/dcode-v62.pdf

## C15 — Real-time curtailment compliance/settlement for non-firm large loads — CLASS C (PASS)
Buyer class: DNOs operating curtailable connections (obligated); non-firm customers (bound by limit).
Budget owner: each DNO licensee, via DCUSA (Access SCR direction) and SLC 31E.
Evidence:
- [V] Ofgem Access SCR Final Decision, 3 May 2022: "Curtailment limits for non-firm connections – the distribution network operator will set curtailment limits and included these in the connection offer to the connecting customer, who will have to abide by those limits. If the network operator needs to curtail above this limit, then they must procure this service from the market." "Non-firm access arrangements will not be available to small users". Reforms "implemented for 01 April 2023". https://www.ofgem.gov.uk/sites/default/files/2022-05/Access%20SCR%20-%20Final%20Decision.pdf
- [V] Access SCR DCUSA Direction (May 2022): DNOs "must raise one or more proposals to modify the DCUSA ... effective as of 1 April 2023"; para 21: parameters incl. "Curtailable Capacity"; "Calculating the number of hours for which a customer has been subject to Curtailment, as the number of hours the customer has been curtailed multiplied by proportion of Curtailable Capacity which was Curtailed ('Curtailment Hours')"; "a limit on the maximum number of Curtailment Hours ('Curtailment Limit') which should: a) be applied in respect of Curtailment Hours over a rolling 12-month period; b) be set by the DNO via a defined process ... c) be applied consistently across all network operators"; "steps the network operator must take in order to avoid exceeding the Curtailment Limit, namely the provision of required network capacity or the procurement of flexibility in line with ... Standard Licence Condition 31E." https://www.ofgem.gov.uk/sites/default/files/2022-05/Access%20SCR%20-%20DCUSA%20Direction1651572952655.pdf
Class A (ANM system procurement): [N] — not pursued after Class C established.
Recurring: YES (rolling 12-month Curtailment Hours accounting per curtailable connection). Large-and-repeatable: per-connection.
Caveats: applies to generation and demand alike (demand not singled out); transmission-level non-firm demand not covered here; the direction mandates the *accounting definition*, and does not itself prescribe real-time telemetry/settlement systems.
