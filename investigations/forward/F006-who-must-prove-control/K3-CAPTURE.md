# F006 — K3 capture, P6 only

Only P6 (aggregate-load accounting against the 300 MW tier) reached
`measured strain`, and weakly: three respondents contest how the
threshold is counted and the government defers to guidance.

**Proposition 1 — the problem is coming.** Yes, dated: Condition 9.6 from
March 2028; NIS designation for ≥ 300 MW around Autumn 2027; an annual
load-capacity RFI.

**Proposition 2 — a buyer is compelled.** Yes: every load controller must
know its aggregate controlled load and notify within three months of
crossing the line.

**Proposition 3 — existing solutions are inadequate for that buyer's
decision at that time.** **Not earned.** The count is produced by the
controller's own platform (device counts and shiftable MW are already
front-page metrics at Axle); the open question is *legal* — whether group
entities aggregate, what counts as "controlled" — and DESNZ has said it
will answer it in guidance. No respondent describes buying, or wanting to
buy, an external count. Recurring spend: none observed.

**K3 verdict: killed — efficiently served / rule pending.** The strain is
standardisation pressure on the rule-writer, resolved by publication, not
by a market.

**No opportunity is named.** Per the declaration: three propositions
earned separately or the honest output is a described problem.
