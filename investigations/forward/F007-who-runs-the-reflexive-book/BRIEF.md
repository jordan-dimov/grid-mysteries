# Whose book it is, what they must do with it by when — and what nobody sells

*One page for a practitioner. Sources are the pinned statutes (EnWG
§ 41a, § 35; MsbG §§ 29–31, 45), the Bundesnetzagentur's rollout figures
of 31 December 2025, the MHHS milestones, the four TSOs' 2024 balancing-
group contract, and the incumbents' and suppliers' own pages, all pinned
by digest in F007. Undated pages carry that caveat. Not published.*

## Whose book

| tariff | who sets the price the battery follows | the supplier's position | who runs it today |
|---|---|---|---|
| **§ 41a pass-through** (every German supplier since 1 Jan 2025; Stadtwerke Aschaffenburg, Solingen, Oerlinghausen, Osnabrück, Merseburg; 1KOMMA5° Dynamic Pulse; dynamicOctopus) | the EPEX day-ahead auction, fixed the day before | **imbalance only**: forecast response to a known price versus actual, settled quarter-hourly; plus the fixed layer (network, levies, Grundpreis) against a moving volume | the balancing-group party: a portfolio manager (Trianel, Syneco, EnBW Energy Factory, Vattenfall) or the supplier's own PFM function |
| **supplier-set price with device control** (Intelligent Octopus Germany, Tibber Smart Battery, Heartbeat AI, Enpal.One; Octopus GB on Kraken; Kraken licensees) | the supplier | the spread between its own price and the market, **closed by control** of the devices | the specialist itself, in-house, since 2023–2025 |
| **GB smart tariffs** (835 000 customers, July 2025; 2.8 % of the market) | the supplier's formula on the day-ahead price, published 16:00 | imbalance on actual half-hourly data from M15; price-and-export arbitrage only where the supplier writes both prices (Octopus, per 010) | the supplier's trading or settlement function; ENSEK / Gentrack / Kraken for the data |

## What must be done, by when

| when | who | what |
|---|---|---|
| since 1 Jan 2025 | every German supplier | offer a dynamic tariff to any customer with an intelligent metering system; inform on "Kosten … Vorteile, Nachteile und Risiken"; hand over a summary of price components (§ 41a(2), (6), (7)) |
| since 18 Dec 2025 (Act) | suppliers > 200 000 customers | also offer a twelve-month fixed-price contract (§ 41a(4)) |
| 31 Dec 2025 → enforcement from 27 Mar 2026 | 813 default metering operators | 20 % of mandatory cases equipped; 23.3 % reached in aggregate, 14.6 % at the 599 smallest; 77 proceedings, Zwangsgeld to follow |
| daily, D-1 after the auction | the balancing-group party | nominate the segment's schedule with "entsprechende Sorgfalt bei der Erstellung der Prognosen"; carry reBAP on the error |
| **28 Oct 2026** | every GB supplier | M14: accept MPANs under the new model or lose the right to take on new customers |
| Nov 2026 | Bundesnetzagentur | first report on "das Marktangebot von Verträgen nach § 41a sowie die Preisvolatilität" (§ 35 No. 10) — the first public count of who holds one |
| **7 May 2027** | every GB supplier | M15: all MPANs migrated; imbalance on actuals |
| 2 Jul 2027 | the GB market | M16: four-month settlement |

## How big the German book can be

3.09 million intelligent metering systems (5.5 % of all metering
locations) and 1.10 million in the mandatory case group that contains
§ 14a batteries, against 2.72 million battery units under 30 kW in
operation and 128 475 batteries already under a § 14a agreement (2024).
The meter binds, but not by an order of magnitude; the specialists
install their own. How many of those households hold a dynamic tariff is
unpublished until November 2026.

## Which of these things nobody currently sells

1. **A response model for a battery fleet on a pass-through tariff, as a
   named service.** Portfolio managers sell forecasting and
   balancing-group management; none names dynamic-tariff or flexible
   household load. Nobody is shown asking. On the pinned evidence the
   generic day-ahead forecast absorbs it.
2. **A bill that explains what the battery earned against the permitted
   pairing.** The vendors bill a time series or an average price
   (cortility for SAP: "ein Durchschnittspreis"); § 41a(7) asks for a
   summary of price components, not an attribution. The specialists show
   it in their apps; no Stadtwerk vendor page offers it. Unforced,
   unbought.
3. **Reconciliation of settlement data to the inverter log.** The meter
   is the legal measure; the complaints route must answer within four
   weeks; the dispute body's 2025 table has no such category. Unforced.
4. **An ex-ante substantiation of "up to € x a month" battery claims.**
   The ASA's four upheld rulings (Aug 2025 – Mar 2026) demand evidence
   that a significant proportion of *actual* customers achieved the
   figure; advertisers meet it from their own billing data. The 010
   computation is not what the regulator asks for.

None of the four has a compelled buyer whose existing solution is shown
to fail. That is why this study names no opportunity.
