**The Stadtwerke class, answered at K0.** For several hundred German
suppliers the § 41a duty produces an import-only pass-through tariff;
the export side of a household battery is the state feed-in tariff paid
outside the supply contract. Function R4 as generated from Investigation
010 (a supplier writing import-and-export pairing rules against its own
customers) does not exist in that class, and the arbitrage 010 computed
is between a supplier's own two prices — a boundary only the specialists
and Octopus have.

**S1, the free reading.** The buyer is real and compelled (four upheld
rulings in seven months), but the substantiation the regulator demands
is *realised* — evidence that "a significant proportion of customers"
achieved the figure — and the advertisers meet it from their own billing
data. The *ex-ante* computation 010 performs (a ceiling against the
permitted pairs and the export connection) is not what the ASA asks for
and nobody is shown buying it. Lender-side substantiation is unobserved.
Not run further.
