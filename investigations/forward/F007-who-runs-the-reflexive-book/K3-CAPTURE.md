# F007 — K3 capture, R5 only

Only R5 (interval billing and explanation) reached `measured strain`,
and weakly: the Schlichtungsstelle records "Umsetzungsschwierigkeiten"
and "erhebliche Bearbeitungs- und Kommunikationsprobleme" in installing
and operating intelligent metering systems and transmitting readings to
suppliers — strain upstream of billing, in the metering-operator chain.

**Proposition 1 — the problem is coming.** Yes, dated: § 41a since
1 January 2025; § 45 quotas (20 % of mandatory cases by 31 December 2025,
enforcement proceedings from 27 March 2026); MHHS M14 28 October 2026 and
M15 7 May 2027.

**Proposition 2 — a buyer is compelled.** Yes: every supplier must bill
what it offers; every default metering operator must install and
transmit; every GB supplier must qualify by M14 or stop taking customers.

**Proposition 3 — existing solutions are inadequate for that buyer's
decision at that time.** **Not earned.** Billing itself is sold by
Schleupen, Wilken, cortility, ENSEK and Gentrack as a release of the
system the buyer already runs. The strained link — the meter — is served
by a formed category: competitive metering operators ("einige Unternehmen
… die neben den grundzuständigen Messstellenbetreibern den Einbau bzw.
den Betrieb von intelligenten Messystemen (iMSys) bundesweit anbieten",
`sse-taetigkeitsbericht-2025-pdf`) and by the specialists installing their
own meters (Octopus Germany, Tibber). No supplier or association is shown
asking for anything a vendor does not sell. The one gap observed — a bill
that explains to a household what its battery earned against the
permitted pairing, rather than an average price — is unforced (§ 41a(7)
asks for a summary of price components) and unbought.

**K3 verdict: killed — efficiently served (billing) / formed category
(metering), rule pending on take-up.** Recurring spend exists and is
already captured by the vendors.

**No opportunity is named.** Per the declaration: three propositions
earned separately or the honest output is a described problem.
