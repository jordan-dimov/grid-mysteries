# Recalibration — 2026-08-26: from generator to verifier

**Decision, by the sponsor, after Batch 02 closed.** Recorded here so it
governs the next month and is not re-argued.

## What Batch 02 settled

`10 → 8 buyer-real → 6 testable → 1 absorption failure → 1 measured strain
→ 0 capturable`. The generator finds real forced functions with real
owners, and the market has almost always already absorbed them. Another
batch now risks turning Grid Mysteries into an elegant machine for
repeatedly proving that mature infrastructure markets are mature.

## The decision

1. **No Batch 03.** The research machinery is frozen where it is:
   Generator v4 unchanged; L15–L17 logged for whenever a BATCH-03
   declaration is written, if ever.
2. **Grid Mysteries' job changes.** It is no longer primarily an
   opportunity generator. It is an **opportunity verifier and killer**.
   Opportunity discovery now comes from people spending money, not from
   public-data patterns:

   > buyer conversation / observed spend / deal → commercial hypothesis →
   > Grid Mysteries falsification → sell

3. **The dated watches stay** because they are cheap: Ofgem's FMAR review
   (Oct 2026); SSEN LRE FD (Q3 2026); Ofgem ED annual report (~Q1 2027);
   NESO PCF activation metric for C2 (1 Feb 2027); Defra MCP registration
   decision and the 1 Jan 2029 deadline; ESO map 90-day read (~24 Nov
   2026); C12/C15 resolving series; F004 reopening triggers.

## The rule for the next month

> **No new opportunity gets more than two hours of desk research until
> somebody in the buyer class has spoken to us. No more than one day of
> work before we know who might write the cheque.**

The lesson it encodes, learned repeatedly this week: a forcing variable
is easy to find; a necessary function is easy to find; strain is
sometimes findable; the rare thing is **unmet pain + budget + willingness
to buy from us**, and that is discovered faster in conversations than in
PDFs.

## The first action

**Five C3 conversations** (Grid Code / EMT model compliance for
inverter-based resources — `F002-batch/KILL-C3.md`), with one question:

> "Tell me about the last model that didn't pass first time. What exactly
> was wrong?"

Brief: `F002-batch/C3-CONVERSATION-BRIEF.md`. Hard milestone: **within two
weeks, obtain evidence that someone would pay for a C3 pre-submission
assurance product or service, or kill C3.**

## Allocation for two weeks

70% commercial discovery on C3 · 20% build only what conversations demand
(if three people independently name the same pre-submission check,
prototype exactly that check — not a platform) · 10% Grid Mysteries
(watches; verification of consequential claims from conversations; no
new batch).

## The scoreboard

Progress is now counted as: buyer conversations · real artefacts obtained
· paid pilots · £ pipeline · £ contracted · repeatable pain discovered.
Not: investigations completed, hypotheses governed, generator accuracy
improved. Those remain supporting capabilities and cannot become the
game.
