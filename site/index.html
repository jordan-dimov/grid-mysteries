Grid Mysteries research

Standing instruments built from Britain's public electricity data, each under a method sealed before its first figure

  • The Balancing Bill
    Who got paid to keep Britain's grid balanced, day by day, from the settlement data the market publishes.
  • GB Connection Slippage
    How far the contract dates in Britain's grid-connection register have moved, in megawatt-years, rebuilt from every copy of the register since 2014.
  • The queue that is past its own date
    The entries in the connection register whose own connection date has passed, counted on each new copy under a method sealed before the count.
  • Where the dates move
    The slippage series cut by plant type and by transmission area, with the margin each identity rule decides shown beside the figure.

Each page is a pure function of evidence committed to a public repository under a declaration frozen before any figure was computed and witnessed by OpenTimestamps and two RFC 3161 authorities. A committed row is never rewritten: a figure that would change is a new declaration beside the old one. Every page says what it never claims. Declarations, evidence, code and tests: github.com/jordan-dimov/grid-mysteries.
